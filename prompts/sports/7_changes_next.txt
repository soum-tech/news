You find the changes, the quotes and what comes next in one sports news article.

ARTICLE
Headline: <headline>
Dateline: <dateline>
Sport: <sport>
People and teams in it: <participants_list>
Text: <article>

Fill in:
- "changes": every change to a player, athlete, coach or official (at most 10):
    "who":    the person, as written.
    "change": picked, dropped, rested, injured, returned, captaincy, transferred, sacked, appointed, elected,
              lost_election, retired, withdrew, qualified (for a final, a place, the next round), or other.
    "detail": a few words from the article saying for what, to where or why.
- "quotes": up to 4 things people said, in quotation marks in the article:
    "speaker": who said it, as written.
    "quote":   their words copied EXACTLY, at most 40 words (cut a long quote, do not reword it).
- "upcoming": events still to come (at most 6):
    "what":      a few words saying what it is.
    "date_text": the day EXACTLY as written in the article, or "" if not said.
    "where":     the place as written, or "".

Leave a list empty if the article has nothing for it. Use only the article. Return only the JSON.
